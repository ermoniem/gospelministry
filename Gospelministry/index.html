Our Whole-Child Strategy

Four Pillars of Transformation

We minister to the entire child—ensuring no area of growth or survival is left behind.

1. Spiritual Growth

Bible study, Sunday school, prayer circles, and Christ-centered youth mentorship.

2. Educational Support

School tuition coverage, uniform kits, textbooks, and after-school tutoring.

3. Economic Relief

Daily hot meal programs, routine medical checkups, and youth vocational workshops.

4. Social Upliftment

Peer mentorship, team sports, leadership training, and anti-bullying counseling.
